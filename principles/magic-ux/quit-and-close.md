# Close hides, Quit stops

- Closing a window only hides it. Only Quit stops the studio.
- There's one Quit. Every path (menu bar, ⌘Q, app menu, Dock, logout) goes to the same stop. The word "Quit" is used nowhere else.
- When nothing is lost, tell people what's happening. Don't ask them to confirm. No "Quit anyway?" dialogs.
- Waiting states are one calm sentence: no spinner, countdown or buttons. Show it only if the wait lasts long enough to notice (about 400 ms).

## Checklist
- [ ] N1 Project creation is a **standalone space** (its own route/page), not a modal. You can leave mid-way, work on other projects, and come back: drafts persist and show on Projects as "In setup" cards to resume. No office yet — a walkthrough (same steps as the popup).
- [ ] N2 At the team step you can **chat with the chosen orchestrator** about the proposal (side chat): ask why, ask for a different team, add/drop a specialist, ask for a new team — the proposal on stage updates from the conversation.
- [ ] N3 Orchestrators propose **whole existing teams first** (one or more teams). Individual agents only as specialists, or for very small projects.
- [ ] N4 **Captain comes from the team's own design** — the orchestrator never picks captains for existing teams. Only when it needs a brand-new team does it (with the Agent Creator) design the team, including its captain.
